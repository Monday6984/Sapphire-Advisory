Fintech

Fintech businesses handle identity documents, bank details, transaction histories and other sensitive personal data from the first customer onwards. Banking partners, payment providers and investors will usually want evidence that you manage this data responsibly before they work with you.

We help fintech startups and scaleups

  • Put clear policies, privacy notices and records of processing in place
  • Prepare for due diligence from partners and investors
  • Assess new features, data uses and AI tools before launch
  • Review supplier and partner contracts
  • Respond to incidents and data subject requests quickly and correctly

Healthtech

Health data is special category data under UK GDPR, which means stricter conditions apply to how you collect, use and share it. If you plan to work with the NHS, you'll usually be expected to complete the Data Security and Protection Toolkit before accessing NHS patient data or systems.

We help healthtech startups and scaleups

  • Complete the DSP Toolkit
  • Carry out DPIAs for products that handle health data
  • Put the right legal bases and safeguards in place for special category data
  • Draft data sharing and data processing agreements with healthcare partners
  • Train clinical and non-clinical staff on handling patient data

Expanding into the UK

UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 set out how organisations must handle personal data in the UK. If you're entering the UK market, you'll need to understand which of these rules apply to you, what documentation you need and whether you need a DPO.

We help businesses expanding into the UK

  • Understand their UK data protection obligations
  • Prepare UK-ready privacy notices, policies and procedures
  • Set up UK DPO support
  • Manage international data transfers
  • Build compliance into their UK launch plan from the start

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions startups ask us most.

Does my business need a Data Protection Officer?

Under UK GDPR, you must appoint a DPO if you're a public authority, if your core activities involve regular and systematic monitoring of people on a large scale, or if you process special category data such as health data on a large scale. Many healthtech businesses fall into this last group. Even where a DPO isn't legally required, having one can make it easier to manage compliance and reassure partners.

What is an outsourced DPO?

An outsourced DPO is an external specialist who carries out the DPO role for your organisation. You get the expertise without the cost of a full-time hire, and the DPO's contact details are notified to the ICO in the usual way.

What should we do if we have a data breach?

Act quickly. If a personal data breach is likely to put people's rights and freedoms at risk, you must report it to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. In some cases you'll also need to tell the people affected. We can help you assess the breach, decide whether it needs reporting and manage the response.

How long do we have to respond to a subject access request?

You usually have one month from receiving the request. This can be extended by up to two further months if the request is complex or you've received several from the same person, as long as you tell them within the first month.

What is a DPIA and when do we need one?

A Data Protection Impact Assessment is a structured way of identifying and reducing the risks of a project that uses personal data. It's required when processing is likely to result in a high risk to individuals, which often includes new technologies, AI, large-scale use of health data and monitoring.

Do you only work with fintech and healthtech businesses?

These are our specialist sectors, but we're happy to speak with any growing business that needs practical data protection support.

We're not based in the UK yet. Can you still help?

Yes. We regularly support businesses planning to enter the UK market and can help you prepare before you launch.
